Frequently asked questions

Are my files uploaded anywhere?
No. All PDF processing happens on your Mac and needs no internet connection. Only starting the trial, buying, restoring purchases and refreshing purchase status connect to Apple's App Store servers.

How does the free trial work?
Start the 7-day free trial from inside the app. It is free and never charges you automatically. After the trial you can unlock export permanently with a one-time purchase. No subscription.

I bought Full Unlock on another Mac. How do I restore it?
Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account and choose Restore Purchases in the app.

Why didn't my file get much smaller?
Compression mostly reduces image-heavy pages (for example scans and photos). Text-only PDFs are often already small. Sendfold never saves a "compressed" file that is larger than the original.

Why can't I export a certain PDF?
To avoid damaging important documents, Sendfold blocks encrypted PDFs, digitally signed PDFs, forms, and PDFs with annotations (web-link annotations are allowed), attachments or scripts. Remove the protection or flatten the file in another app first.

Does it overwrite my original?
No. Sendfold always saves a new copy.

I found a hidden ".sendfold-….tmp" file in my output folder. What is it?
While saving, Sendfold writes a hidden temporary file in the folder you chose and then replaces it with the finished PDF. If a save was interrupted (for example by a crash), the file may be left behind. You can safely delete it.

What do I need?
A Mac running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
