Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Sendfold does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Your PDFs never leave your Mac.

1. Who we are

Sendfold is a macOS app developed by Ofer Lazmi, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the app handles information.

2. Your documents stay on your Mac

  • All merging, page editing and compression happens locally on your Mac.
  • The app works fully offline. It does not upload your files to us or to any server.
  • The app only opens files and folders you explicitly choose (macOS App Sandbox, user-selected file access).
  • Output files are saved only where you choose. Temporary working files are kept inside the app's own sandbox container on your Mac.

3. No data collection, no tracking

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No personal data, usage data, identifiers or diagnostics are sent to us.
  • We do not sell or share any data, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

4. Purchases

The free trial and the one-time Full Unlock are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We never receive your payment details, name or Apple ID. The app only checks Apple-signed transaction records on your Mac to know whether export is unlocked. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Data stored on your Mac

The app keeps small local settings (for example your preferences and trial/purchase status) on your Mac. This information stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app and its data.

6. Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

7. Contacting support

If you email us, we receive only what you choose to send (for example your email address and message) and use it only to answer you. You can ask us to delete that correspondence at any time.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
